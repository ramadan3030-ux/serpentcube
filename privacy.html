Serpent Cube Privacy Policy

Published by Ramadan Digital · Last updated 26 November 2026

Serpent Cube is a game you play on your own device. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. This policy explains the small amount of data involved in showing ads and handling the optional “Remove ads” purchase.

Data the game keeps on your device

Your scores, unlocked worlds and sound settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows an ad between games. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ad and how you interacted with it, plus diagnostics such as crashes.

On iPhone you are asked whether to allow tracking, and you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

If you buy “Remove ads”, the payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details. Our purchase service, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt so it can confirm and restore your purchase. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

We do not sell personal data, and Serpent Cube does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Buying “Remove ads” turns off ads and the advertising data collection that comes with them.

Children

Serpent Cube is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
